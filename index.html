Our Mission

Why APU Wellness Fest?

This imaginary student event is designed to encourage students to develop healthier physical, mental, and social habits while building a stronger campus community.

University life is intellectually demanding. APU Wellness Fest 2026 creates a welcoming, judgment-free environment where students can pause, reset their circadian rhythms, learn nutritious habits, and engage in energizing physical pursuits alongside peers and mentors.
