Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating, scheduling, rules, and student volunteer opportunities.

Yes! APU Wellness Fest 2026 is 100% free of charge for all currently enrolled students, university faculty, and campus staff. Admission includes access to all physical challenges, workshops, food exhibition tasting booths, and guided mindfulness sessions.

All APU students (Foundation, Diploma, Undergraduate, and Postgraduate) across every faculty are welcome and warmly encouraged to participate. Staff, faculty members, and student alumni are also invited to attend community showcases.

Yes, online registration via our Registration page is required to issue your unique Digital Event Pass. This pass grants you priority entry into limited-capacity workshops, meal coupon distribution, and participation in competitive events.

Absolutely! You may register for and join as many activities across the 3 days as your schedule permits, provided timings do not overlap. Many students combine morning yoga with afternoon nutrition workshops and the 5K Wellness Walk.

All festival activities take place across the LBEF Campus grounds located in Maitidevi, Kathmandu (Opposite Maitidevi Temple), in collaboration with APU. Specific zones include the Campus Atrium, Seminar Hall, Fitness Ground, and Open Courtyard.

Yes. Non-competing spectators and student supporters are welcome to cheer on peers in the fitness challenges, browse the Vegan Food Exhibition, visit sponsor booths, and attend open keynote talks.

Volunteer applications are open via our News page or by contacting our committee via the Contact form. Over 20 student volunteers assist with participant check-in, obstacle timekeeping, media production, and green campus recycling.

Yes! All attendees who check into at least 2 sessions with their digital pass receive a certified digital e-Certificate of Participation issued to their student email. Volunteer marshals and challenge podium winners receive framed certificates of achievement.
